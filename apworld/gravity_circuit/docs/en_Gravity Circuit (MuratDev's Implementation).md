# Gravity Circuit

## What does randomization do to this game?
Every Circuit boss, rescue bot, health/burst booster and palette chip is a check. Stages are locked on the
stage select until you receive their Access item. Boss Burst Techniques and booster chips are items instead of
shop unlocks.

## What is the goal?
Open the Fortress by defeating the required number of Circuit bosses, then beat the final boss in Fortress 3.

## Which items can be in another player's world?
Stage Access items, the 16 boss Burst Techniques, booster chips 2–20 (Air Jumper included), Health and Burst
Upgrades, armor paints and credit bundles.

## What does another world's item look like in Gravity Circuit?
The pickup behaves normally on screen, and the overlay in the top-left says what was sent and to whom.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
It is applied right away and announced in the overlay. Stage Access shows up when you open the stage select.
